Privacy Policy

Privacy information for our mobile games, applications and related services.

Last Updated: September 25, 2026

1. Introduction

THE GAME KINGDOMS (SMC-PRIVATE) LIMITED is a Pakistan-based mobile game development and software publishing company. We develop Android mobile games and mobile applications for users across international markets. This Privacy Policy explains the general categories of information that may be handled when you use our games, applications, websites and related services.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on the game, application, device, platform and features you use, information may include device and technical information such as device model, operating system, language, approximate region, application version, advertising identifiers, IP address, crash information and gameplay or diagnostic information. We may also receive information that you voluntarily provide when you contact us for support.

3. Advertising and Analytics

Our games may use third-party advertising, analytics or mediation services. These services may collect or process device, advertising, usage and diagnostic information in accordance with their own privacy policies. The specific third-party services used may vary by game and may change as our applications are updated.

4. How We Use Information

Information may be used to provide and maintain our games and applications, improve gameplay and performance, understand usage and technical issues, deliver relevant advertising where applicable, prevent abuse or fraud, provide customer support and develop or improve our products and services.

5. Software Publishing and Third-Party Platforms

Our Android games and applications may be distributed through Galaxy Store and other supported digital app stores. When you download or use an application through a third-party platform, that platform may independently process information under its own terms and privacy policy.

6. Children's Privacy

Our services are not intended to knowingly collect personal information directly from children in violation of applicable laws. If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that we can review the request and take appropriate action.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, to operate our services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes and enforce applicable agreements.

9. Your Choices and Requests

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights concerning personal information processed about you. You may also be able to manage advertising or privacy settings through your device, application store or relevant third-party service.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, technologies or legal requirements change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
