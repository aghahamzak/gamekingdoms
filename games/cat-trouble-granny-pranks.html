Cat Trouble: Granny Pranks

Kitty Mischief House Simulator
View on Apple App Store All Games

Overview

Cat Trouble: Granny Pranks is a playful mobile cat simulation adventure where players take control of a mischievous kitty exploring a house filled with interactive objects, challenges and prank opportunities.

Players can explore different rooms, interact with household objects, create funny situations and complete missions while trying to avoid getting caught by granny.

Gameplay

Explore bedrooms, kitchens, living spaces and other areas of the house while searching for useful objects and new opportunities to create mischief.

Players can knock over and move objects, discover hidden areas, complete different objectives and use the environment to escape when granny comes looking for the naughty pet.

The gameplay combines exploration, stealth, object interaction and casual mission-based challenges.

Key Features

  • Play as a mischievous kitty
  • Interactive house environments
  • Multiple rooms to explore
  • Household object interactions
  • Prank and mischief gameplay
  • Stealth and escape challenges
  • Mission-based objectives
  • Hide and avoid granny
  • Casual exploration gameplay
  • Simple mobile controls
